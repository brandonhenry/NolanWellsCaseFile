Plain-language overview · July 3–6, 2026

What happened to Nolan Wells?

Nolan Wells, 18, was reported missing after a July 4 gathering at Horn Island, Mississippi. His body was recovered near the island’s northwestern end on the morning of July 6. This summary explains what the available public evidence supports—and what it does not establish.

Published and last reviewed July 28, 2026

Short answer

The public record establishes the trip, several vessel movements, the search, and the recovery. It does not establish what happened to Nolan during the final unresolved period on Horn Island.

The narrowest defensible reconstruction places the critical gap between approximately 3:45 PM and 4:31 PM on July 4. The precise order of the altercation, circulated video, Sea Tow call, witness departures, and Nolan’s last known movements has not been independently authenticated minute by minute.

Witness and media sequence

What is reported, but not independently timed to the minute

  1. Before 11:14 AM: Tracestin Shepherd says Nolan called for help locating where to anchor. The approximate time is based on his public recollection.
  2. Early afternoon: Nolan reportedly swims and socializes among the boats. A circulated photograph supports his presence but does not supply an authenticated capture time.
  3. Around 3:00 PM: Warren says this was about the last time he personally saw Nolan. This is a witness estimate, not an official timestamp.
  4. Around 3:30 PM: Tracestin says he was involved in an altercation and was forced back aboard his family’s separate boat.
  5. Around 4:00 PM: A released distress call reports bilge-pump failure, water ingress, and approximately seven people aboard the distressed vessel.
  6. Around 4:01 PM, reported: The circulated Horn Island video records Tracestin yelling. The original device file and native metadata are not public.
  7. Before 4:31 PM: Public witness accounts say Nolan remained on or near the island and did not leave with one group. The exact words, time, and complete listener set remain unresolved.

Central evidence gap

What remains unknown between approximately 3:45 PM and 4:31 PM

  • Nolan’s precise location and movements throughout the interval.
  • The authenticated minute-by-minute order of the altercation, video, distress call, and departures.
  • The complete passenger manifest for each relevant vessel at each stage.
  • Who personally heard Nolan’s reported decision to remain and the exact words used.
  • Whether additional original video, phone, dispatch, GPS, or witness records resolve the gap.

Search and recovery

July 4–6

Nolan’s family was contacted later on July 4 after friends realized he had not returned. Search activity expanded on July 5. Nolan’s body was recovered from the water near Horn Island’s northwestern end on the morning of July 6. The publicly reported recovery time is approximate in this archive unless tied to a specific obtained official entry.

Editorial caution

This archive does not determine criminal or civil responsibility. It reports the available public record and marks uncertainty. A witness statement is evidence of what the witness publicly said; it is not automatically independent proof that every detail occurred exactly as recalled.
