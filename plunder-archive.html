Rights, provenance, and limits

Permission does not remove the privacy review.

The site owner reports that PLUNDER granted permission to redistribute this material. The archive preserves that provenance while applying a second, file-level check for residential addresses, identification records, signatures, and unrelated third-party dispatch content. Publication here means a file passed that check; it does not authenticate every statement inside it.
