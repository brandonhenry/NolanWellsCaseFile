Public post attributes a four-image message sequence to Katie Hudson Seymour

Posted by Boogie LowDown · conversation claimed July 5, 2026 around 11:30 PM · reviewed July 30, 2026

Post caption context
The poster identifies the screenshots as a conversation with Warren Hudson’s mother, Katie Hudson Seymour, concerning Warren, Nolan, Wyatt, and the Horn Island incident. The caption dates the contact to July 5, 2026 and says it occurred “at around 11:30PM.”

Claims visible in the screenshots

  • The sender shown as “Katie Hudson Seymour” says Wyatt repeatedly asked Nolan to board “at five when it was time to leave.”
  • The sender says Nolan wanted to remain with a girl and that the girl’s boat was parked next to Wyatt’s.
  • The sender says Wyatt’s boat began filling with water, broke down, and was towed.
  • The sender says Warren neither traveled out nor returned with Nolan, later noticed Nolan was missing at a friend’s house, and contacted Nolan’s mother, the Coast Guard, and the sheriff’s office.
  • The sender says she had spent the day looking for Nolan and asks the poster to stop publishing about Warren and the family.

Assessment: the public post, caption context, four attachments, displayed conversation name, and displayed “SUN AT 11:26 PM” marker are directly preserved. The images are material evidence of what the post publicly alleged the sender communicated. Screenshots alone do not authenticate the account holder, sender identity, phone ownership, complete conversation, message-date year, or truth and precision of the underlying claims. The reported 5:00 PM request is therefore added to the timeline as a low-confidence secondhand claim, not a confirmed timestamp; it is not reconciled with MI4088BU’s verified 4:31 PM movement.

Read the preserved message context

The first message identifies the sender as Warren’s mother, disputes the poster’s prior information, says Warren did not ride out or return with Nolan, and states that Nolan was supposed to board Wyatt’s boat.

It continues that Wyatt repeatedly asked Nolan to board “at five when it was time to leave”; Nolan allegedly wanted to stay with a girl whose boat was next to Wyatt’s; Wyatt allegedly asked several times in the hearing of others; Wyatt’s boat then allegedly took on water, broke down, and was towed.

The sender says Warren was not on that boat, says Warren later noticed Nolan was missing after returning to a friend’s house, and says he contacted Nolan’s mother, the Coast Guard, and the sheriff’s office. The sender also describes briefly seeing Warren during a short trip with another son, says the family had been searching all day, and repeatedly asks the poster to remove posts and photographs about the family.

The final visible blue message from the recipient asks whether the information and a description of the girl had been provided to law enforcement.

What the ledger does not treat as evidence

Questions, prayers, blame, unsupported theories, unrelated images, altered accusation graphics, and visual guesses without authenticated media are omitted. Reposts of later articles do not become new sources. A caption expressing distrust does not authenticate an attached photo. Repeated screenshots of one comment thread remain one source chain.
