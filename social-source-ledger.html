What the ledger does not treat as evidence

A screenshot interpreting a distant wave or pixel as a person is preserved for provenance but is not probative. Reposts of later articles do not become new sources. A caption expressing distrust or a theory does not authenticate the attached photo. Repeated screenshots of one comment thread remain one source chain.
